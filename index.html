How to Play Gridfire Arena

Gridfire Arena is a single-player deathmatch. You and a crew of computer-controlled bots share one arena. Every time you eliminate a bot, your score goes up. Reach 25 eliminations to win the match, and try to do it faster every time.

Game mode and difficulty

The match ends when you reach 25 eliminations. If a bot defeats you, you respawn a couple of seconds later at the safest spawn point with full health and ammo. Health packs glow in several spots around the arena and come back after a short time. Your health also slowly recovers if you stay out of damage for a few seconds.

  • Easy: five bots with slower reactions and weak aim. A relaxed way to learn the arena.
  • Normal: seven bots that react quickly and hit more often. The recommended starting point.
  • Hard: nine fast, accurate bots. Expect to be flanked.

Your best time for each difficulty is saved in your browser, so you can chase a new personal record.

Weapons in Gridfire Arena

Three weapons cover close, medium and long range. Switch with the number keys depending on the fight.

1. Pulse Rifle

An automatic all-rounder. Hold the fire button for steady damage at any range. Burst your shots to keep accuracy high.

2. Scatter Cannon

Fires a spread of energy pellets. Devastating up close, weak from far away. Use it in corridors and around corners.

3. Rail Lance

A slow, precise, high-damage shot that can eliminate a bot in one hit. Line up the crosshair and make every shot count.

Tips to Win Faster

  • Use cover. The pillars and walls break line of sight. Peek, shoot, then step back.
  • Keep moving. Strafing makes you much harder to hit. Standing still is the fastest way to lose health.
  • Watch the radar. The small map in the corner shows nearby bots so you can ambush them.
  • Pick the right weapon. Scatter Cannon for tight spaces, Rail Lance for long lanes, Pulse Rifle for everything else.
  • Reload in safety. Reloading takes time. Do it behind a wall, not in the open.
  • Grab health packs. A quick detour to a pack is often better than pushing with low health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gridfire Arena free to play?

Yes. Gridfire Arena is completely free. There is no download, no account and no payment.

Does Gridfire Arena work on mobile?

The game is built for keyboard and mouse, so it is best played on a desktop or laptop computer.

Is Gridfire Arena multiplayer?

Right now you play against computer-controlled bots. You choose Easy, Normal or Hard.

How do I win a match?

Eliminate 25 bots before you fall behind. Collect health packs and use cover to stay alive.

Can I change the mouse sensitivity?

Yes. Use the sensitivity slider on the start screen. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

Which browsers can run the game?

Any modern desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

About Gridfire Arena

Gridfire Arena is an independent browser game with its own original arena, characters, weapons and sounds. It uses no external libraries and loads from a single page, so it starts quickly even on modest computers. The game stores only your settings and best times, locally in your browser.
